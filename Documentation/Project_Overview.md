# Global Electronics Retailer — Business Intelligence & Portfolio Overview

## Executive Summary
This end-to-end Business Intelligence project provides an omnichannel commercial and operational audit for a multi-national consumer electronics retailer operating across 8 countries, 66 physical retail outlets, and a global digital ecommerce storefront.

The solution encompasses the entire modern data lifecycle:
1. Ingestion and cleansing of over **62,880 transaction records**, **15,260 customer profiles**, and **2,510 product SKUs**.
2. Construction of an optimized star schema semantic model with high-performance VertiPaq tabular modeling.
3. 17 production-grade DAX measures spanning base iterators, financial ratios, time-intelligence, and customer loyalty algorithms.
4. A 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard featuring **32 meticulously aligned visuals**.
5. An enterprise SQL companion analysis comprising **35 business questions** executed with exact numerical verification.

## Business Problem & Objectives
Prior to this project, leadership lacked cross-channel visibility into:
- Category margin differentials between high-volume accessories and high-ticket hardware.
- International store footprint efficiency ($/m²) to identify lease renewal opportunities.
- Ecommerce fulfillment lead times and their correlation with repeat customer retention.
- Demographic purchasing patterns across gender and age brackets.

This project delivers actionable operational intelligence addressing every objective with validated data.
